# Provenance and redistribution status

This engineering fork derives from [hangenba/enc_dashformer](https://github.com/hangenba/enc_dashformer),
and from subsequent local optimisation work. The local upstream-derived baseline
was commit `6724285`; the public upstream snapshot checked on 23 September 2026
was `9aa5d9ea404a5d6a62add4f3c1569a536bc51f94`.
Credit for the original encrypted Dashformer implementation belongs to its authors.
The Go dependency licenses remain those of their respective authors; dependencies
are fetched through Go modules and are not vendored here.

No standalone license file was found in the inspected upstream snapshot. This
repository therefore does not assign a new blanket license to inherited code.
Publication of this engineering fork does not establish permission for unrestricted
redistribution. Upstream licensing must be clarified before advertising a fully
licensed open-source release. Competition publication terms are not silently
substituted for a repository license.

Competition inputs, trained model files, downloaded papers, unrelated research
projects, and unpublished manuscripts are excluded from this repository. Obtain
model assets and data separately under their applicable terms.
